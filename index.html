What the system is actually doing

Gold/Silver live prices are separate from macro data. Historical daily closes drive RSI, EMA, MACD and ATR. On Saturday/Sunday the dashboard marks the market closed and shows no new daily percentage change; Monday resumes from the latest available prior session. This prevents a weekend from being treated as a zero-return trading day.
